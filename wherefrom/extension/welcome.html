One more step: install wherefrom

This extension tells the wherefrom program on your computer where each download came from. wherefrom keeps the record, and shows it when you right-click a file and choose Where did this come from?

Checking…

  1. Install wherefrom

    It's free and open source, and needs no admin rights.

    Get wherefrom

  2. Come back here

    This page notices on its own once wherefrom is installed, or use Check again above. Downloads you make in the meantime wait in the extension and are recorded then.

What the extension sees

Only finished downloads: the file's location on your computer, the link it came from, the page that link was on, its type and size. Never the pages you visit, and nothing from private windows. It's sent only to wherefrom on this computer; nothing goes over the internet. You can pause it or skip sites from the toolbar button.
